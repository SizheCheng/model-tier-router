# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect privacy, accept technical
review in good faith, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or
personally identifying content. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions
and restrict participation when behavior threatens safety or collaboration.

Before public hosting, the repository owner must publish a private conduct
reporting channel. Reports should include relevant context and must be handled
confidentially and without retaliation.
